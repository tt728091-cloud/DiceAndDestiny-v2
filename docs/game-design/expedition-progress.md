## M4 — Ambush, Fortified, Lieutenant buffs, Conqueror (2026-10-12)

Engine (`internal/battle/engine/settled_engine.go`, `legal_actions.go`, `state/settled.go`, `snapshot/snapshot.go`):
- Statuses are read by their `modifiers` fields, never by ID (`statusModifierTotal`, `removeStatusesWith`).
- **Ambush**: the first planning roll adds one bonus die per stack (`rollBonusDice`, temporary dice of the loadout's die), the status is used up, and `SettledActorRuntime.AmbushChoose` (= the loadout size) opens the choice: the only legal command is `planning_keep` with exactly that many indices (56 keeps for 8 dice); any other command is refused with "choose exactly 5 dice to keep first". The keep collapses the tray onto owned dice 0..4, keeping faces, uses no roll, records the choice in the roll history, and normal keep/reroll resumes (max 3 rolls). Unused Ambush expires at Offensive exit (`expireFirstRollBonuses`). The snapshot's dice state exposes `choose_count`.
- **Fortified / Conqueror**: `rollSelectedDefense` adds `defense_dice_bonus` × stacks dice; every face feeds its outcome.
- **Lieutenant's Favor**: `resolvedOffensiveOperations` appends `attack_damage_bonus` × stacks damage to any damaging attack (a miss stays a miss).

## M9 — Grouped wounds, camp healing, the rest draws the hand (2026-10-12)

Two reports from the user's play-through drove this milestone.

1. **Wounds.** *"A wound represents a grouping of damage; a wound of zero damage does not exist."* The battle already tracks it that way ("Wound 1 · 6 damage · Round 5 · Brine Lash · Cards lost: …"), but the map counted every lost card as a wound, so a 6-damage hit became six wounds and the old camp mending (one d6 roll per bandage or 3 Influence, success on 4+, inn +1) rolled per card. Asked for: the map keeps the battle's groupings; at camp every character has basic first aid — per untreated wound roll 1d3 and heal that many points; a wound healed to zero goes away, otherwise it is treated and basic aid cannot be used on it again that camp; bandages then give treated wounds a second attempt at 1d6 each; after that nothing more heals a wound at that camp except special abilities; the next camp resets everything and every wound gets another attempt. The inn adds +1 to each healing roll. Site mending (village 2 Influence, monastery 1, town 15 gold) is unchanged but heals one point of damage (one card) per payment. A normal defeat is one defeat wound of 2 damage (`defeat.wounds` is the damage), never the last live card; an event that costs a wound inflicts one wound named after the event.
2. **The rest.** *The user resumed a save and saw "Camp · deck empty" while the draw pile showed 5 cards and the hand 1/4.* The round still ends when the draw pile is empty at the end of a turn (one pass through the deck is one round — the Warlord's clock), but the rest had already reshuffled Spent into the draw pile while the hand stayed as the turn left it, so the camp sat on a near-empty hand beside a full pile and its title explained nothing. Resolution: the rest reshuffles Spent, resets energy **and draws the fresh hand immediately**, so the camp shows the full hand; the client titles it "Camp · the deck ran out" and explains "Round N is over: your draw pile ran out at the end of turn N". Break camp no longer draws (camps saved before this change draw at break camp once, via `CampState.hand_drawn`). Consequence: cards played for Influence at camp come out of the round's opening hand (the same cost as Influence at a village), so camp deck changes are reachable; a chosen camp forfeits the unplayed hand into the reshuffle and still gets a fresh hand.

## Decisions made during implementation

1. **Tile stack and the Warlord's goal.** The region's slots are typed (countryside or core). Countryside tiles form a seeded stack drawn in order into any empty countryside slot the player explores. Core tiles are dealt (seeded shuffle) to the core slots when the region is built, and a core slot can be explored only once the countryside stack is empty ("countryside tiles first, then core tiles"). Reason: the Warlord's route must be "laid out at region build" ~5 hexes from his goal, which is a site on a core tile; dealing the core tiles at build is the only way the goal hex exists before it is explored. The player never learns a core slot's tile before exploring it (viewer-safe status omits it).
2. **Group adjacency is a static content rule for open groups.** The region loader checks every pair of tiles in every possible relative flower offset (and within each tile): no hex may touch two different *open* groups (groups standing on the map, which provoke). In practice this means open groups sit at tile centres. Garrisons, occupiers and guardians wait inside their sites and fight only when the player enters, so they never pull a second group into a battle. Patrol steps are guarded at run time: a step that would bring a group within two hexes of another living open group, onto the player, a site or impassable terrain is skipped (the group waits). This makes "never place two groups so that one hex touches both" a load-time guarantee independent of the seed.
3. **Movement is one hex per `move` action.** The authority moves one adjacent hex at a time (so provoke detection is exact); the payment (cards and/or energy) converts into this turn's Move pool, the entered hex's cost is charged from the pool, and leftover Move is lost at end of turn. The client may chain steps along a path it displays, but every step is validated by the authority and the status lists each adjacent hex's cost and whether it provokes.
4. **Influence is a per-turn, per-site pool.** Cards played at a site (`use_site` with `cards`) go to Spent and add 1 Influence each to `turn.influence`, valid only at that site this turn; services (mending, deck changes) consume it. This is literally "cards played at a site this turn" and lets the Prepare screen reuse `deck_change` without choosing cards inside Prepare.
5. **Rest is an explicit camp phase.** Rest steps 1–2 (reshuffle Spent, energy reset) run immediately; the save enters `phase: camp` where camp services are offered (`camp_service`, `deck_change`); `break_camp` runs steps 4–5 (Warlord march, patrols, Day↔Night, draw) and writes a rest summary into the state for the client. After the last round the Warlord deadline fight starts on `break_camp`.
6. **Pending battles are persisted in the save.** A fight (challenge, provoke, garrison, deadline) writes a `pending_battle` context (opponent, count, kind, ambush, fortified, energy brought in, origin hex, seed). The client initializes the runtime with that opponent and calls `reset` with the save; the session records the result once when terminal. Quitting mid-battle keeps the pending battle; relaunching restarts that battle from its start with the same seed (the learned runtime keeps battles in memory only, as the existing campaign does). Recorded as a limitation of criterion 2 (mid-battle = identical battle start, not identical mid-battle progress).
7. **Statuses carry their numbers as stacks.** Fortified and Conqueror add one defensive die per stack; Ambush adds one bonus die per stack on the first offensive roll; the Lieutenant buff adds one damage per stack to every damaging attack. `expedition.yaml` holds the stack counts (`fortified.extra_defense_dice`, `conqueror.extra_defense_dice`, `ambush.bonus_dice`, one Lieutenant-buff stack per living Lieutenant), so one number lives in one place and the status bar shows it as the count. `ambush.max_kept` must equal the five-dice loadout (validated), because the engine collapses the first roll back to the loadout size.
8. **Ambush first roll = roll 5+3, then choose exactly 5.** After the eight-dice first roll the only legal planning command is `planning_keep` with exactly five indices, which collapses the tray to those five (consuming no roll); the normal keep/reroll flow then continues (max 3 rolls). Keeping K and rerolling 5−K fresh dice is equivalent to choosing any five and rerolling 5−K of them, so this matches "keep at most 5; each reroll returns to 5 dice total" without letting any other effect address a sixth die. Ambush expires at the end of round 1's Offensive segment if it was never used (stated in its rules text).
9. **Map cards are blank battle cards with a `map_effect`.** A card with `map_effect` and no program/mechanic is a blank card (never legal in battle, still health, removable by damage); "both" cards keep their program. No new engine gate is needed beyond the existing blank-card behaviour, which the tests assert explicitly.
10. **The Warlord never provokes.** He marches on an agenda; the player challenges him from an adjacent hex. Provoke detection applies to minion groups only, so a hex that touches a minion group and the Warlord never pulls two enemies into one fight. Minion groups are validated statically (decision 2). If his route crosses a hex holding a minion group he shares it; if it is the player's hex he waits one step short.
11. **Entering a site is the action and costs no Move.** `enter_site` from an adjacent hex moves the player onto the keep/tower/ruins/grotto/occupied-village hex and starts the garrison or guardian fight; a defeat pushes the player back to the hex they came from. Event sites trigger when the player moves onto them (Slay-the-Spire "?" rooms) and must be resolved before any other action; each is one-shot.
13. **The Warlord's route avoids slot centres.** His BFS route (laid out at build) stays on the region's tiles and off every slot centre except his goal, so he never stands where an open group stands; he waits a step short of the player or of a group on his road. Patrols never step onto his hex.
14. **A rest after a fight.** A fight ends the turn; if the end-of-turn draw empties the draw pile, the usual rest follows immediately (reshuffle, energy reset, camp). The battle result panel therefore may show a camp.
15. **Mended cards return to the draw pile** at a seeded random position, so mending is useful before the next rest; bought cards go on top of the draw pile.
16. **Ambush keeps the five-dice loadout**: the engine collapses the first roll back to `LoadoutDiceCount` (5); `ambush.max_kept` must equal it.
12. **Deck changes keep copies in place.** An upgrade/trade-down replaces the copy in hand first, then draw pile, then Spent; a sold or stored copy leaves Spent first, then draw, then hand; a bought card goes on top of the draw pile (it adds health and time to this round, as the design says). Wounded copies never move.

17. **The map clears the hex selection after every successful action**, so the next click always selects (a second click on the selected hex deselects it).
18. **The rest summary is shown before the fight the deadline brings** (overlay order: battle result → rest summary → pending fight → event → event outcome → camp → region end), so the player reads what the last rest did before the Warlord's fight card appears.
19. **Event outcomes are identified by site, event, round and turn** (`last_event.site/round/turn`), so dismissing one does not re-show it when the turn number changes and a later event at another site shows fresh.
20. **The expedition battle result panel is three lines** (result + rewards; piles back/energy kept; early strike/region won) so the Continue button stays inside the result panel without scrolling at 1920×1080.
21. **Test proxies play only legal actions.** The map pointer test's heuristic human (rolls, rerolls toward the strongest symbol, picks the highest-damage qualified tier, defends with the guard ability, plays prevention cards) is a test helper; the authority still lists and validates every command. Seeds are probed through the runtime ops, never by reading hidden state.
22. **A draw records nothing.** Both sides falling in the same round restores the pre-battle piles, wounds and energy, awards nothing and leaves the enemy; the turn ends. In the forced deadline fight the Warlord attacks again immediately (the fight cannot be escaped). This follows the linear campaign's "defeat or draw keeps the pre-battle wounds" rule and keeps the player from ending with no live card.
23. **The placeholder Warlord is tuned to be beatable** (18 health, 4/6/8 damage, prevents 1/2/3) so the victory ending is reachable and testable. Real balance is for playtesting, per the user.
24. **The map's look is "The Surveyor's Chart"**: a parchment chart on the battle's stone table, ink and watercolour tiles, bone chips, gold and red pieces, a stone ledger with parchment cards, and bone-framed overlays over habitat paintings — chosen by a judged design pass for readability and fit with the battle screen. No hand-painted tile art; the habitat paintings back overlays only (they are night scenes and would muddy hexes).
25. **Information is a separate ungraded layer.** Night grades only the paper and tiles; chips, chits, pieces, rings and labels are drawn by the marks layer above the vignette, so gold, red and ivory keep their saturation. Fog is four materials (live, sepia, pencil + "?", bare vellum), never four brightnesses.
26. **Wounds are groupings of damage.** A map wound is the cards one damage source removed together (a battle hit, a defeat, an event), as the battle's wound ledger already records it; its damage is the cards still in it, and a wound healed to zero is dropped. Health lost is the number of wounded cards, never the number of wounds. Saves written with a flat list of card ids load as one wound named "earlier wounds" (so do `pending_battle.wounds_before` and wounds carried into a new expedition).
27. **The rest draws the fresh hand; break camp does not.** `beginRest` reshuffles Spent, resets energy and draws up to hand size before the camp opens, so the camp never shows an empty hand beside a full draw pile and Influence at camp comes out of that hand (the round's opening hand, the same cost as at a village). The round still ends when the draw pile is empty at the end of a turn. Camps saved before this change (`hand_drawn: false`) draw once at break camp.
28. **Camp healing is basic aid, then a bandage, once each per wound per camp.** Basic aid (free, 1d3) on an untreated wound marks it treated; a bandage (1d6, spends one from the pack) on a treated wound marks it bandaged; nothing else heals at that camp. The flags reset when the next rest begins and stay visible between camps, so the view can say which wounds were treated at the last one. Site mending heals one point (one card) per payment.
29. **A defeat is one wound.** A normal defeat inflicts one wound "defeat by <enemy>" of `defeat.wounds` damage (2), never the last live card, on top of the restored pre-battle wounds; an event that costs a wound inflicts one wound named after the event. `defeat.wounds` is damage, not a count.
