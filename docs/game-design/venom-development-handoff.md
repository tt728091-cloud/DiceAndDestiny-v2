## Decisions that must survive

- Needlefang: 3 Fang → 4 direct + 1 Poison; 4 Fang → 3 direct + 2 Poison; 5 Fang → 2 direct + 3 Poison. Any qualified tier may be chosen. User valued normalized 6/7/8 damage based on an unmodified Poison stack’s expected lifetime damage of 2. Delayed damage is not identical in tempo or reliability to direct damage.
- Terminal Bite was redesigned twice. Final: **Gain 1 Catalyst, then deal 4 damage and Provoke 2 against that enemy.** Gain is before the attack; checks are after the attack. Do not revert to consuming toxins for +2 damage or non-clearing bonus rolls.
- Catalyst is automatic. Cap 3; at most one spend per holder per toxin batch after ordinary roll responses. Prioritize an enemy Volatile Poison showing 6, otherwise normal Poison showing 5–6. Never reroll Volatile 5. Do not protect the holder’s own toxins. Each die permits one physical reroll across Venom effects; rerolled results can still clear. September 8 correction: already-collected rolls remain eligible after cleansing, because their outcomes still resolve; do not require a live toxin stack.
- Incubation’s original delayed-Poison design was rejected. Final marker upgrades one surviving normal Poison into Volatile Poison after the next Ongoing toxin checks and damage, then expires. Cap 1; no refresh; only the captured original marker matures, not a replacement. Volatile capacity matters at conversion, not application.
- Any normal Poison overflow from Venom proposes 1 Incubation if none exists. Merely reaching cap 3 without excess does not count. A full Volatile cap does not prevent Incubation. Recheck normal Poison/no marker when the ordinary application commits.
- Shedskin: roll two dice, each Fang prevents 1; each Gland gains 1 Catalyst; any Coil applies one Incubation to the attacker if absent. This Coil explicitly works with no Poison already present. Optional payment of 1 Catalyst before rolling prevents another 2.
- Barbed Mantle: the user swapped Gland and Coil. Gland prevents 3 and gains 1 Catalyst. Coil prevents 1 and applies Incubation if normal Poison exists and no marker, otherwise applies 1 Poison. Fang prevents 2 and applies 1 Poison. Cost 1 energy, incoming Offensive ability only.
- **Agitate (updated September 8):** Choose Poison or Volatile Poison on the enemy and check every existing stack of that chosen type. Independent of the shared Provoke allowance, with normal damage/recovery and Catalyst rules. Cost stays 1 Energy. This supersedes the earlier Provoke 1 implementation.
- Provoke checks existing distinct Poison/Volatile stacks with their normal damage and clearing results. Shared maximum two bonus checks per target per round. Cards reserve at acceptance; abilities capture after final tier/target revalidation and late cancellation. Ordinary checks and Catalyst retries do not spend this allowance.
- Venom Gland: 2 Gland + 1 Coil → 1 Poison + 2 Catalyst. Fever Spike: lesser 2 Fang + 1 Gland + 1 Coil → 3 damage/Provoke 1; greater 2 Fang + 2 Gland + 1 Coil → 4 damage/Provoke 2.
