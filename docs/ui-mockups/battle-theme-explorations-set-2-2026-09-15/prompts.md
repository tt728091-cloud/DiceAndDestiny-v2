Use distinct themed shapes, materials, typography and color for the UI as well as the environment. Generous negative space, roughly half screen free of panels, visual hierarchy, comfortably readable contrast. Retain all required information without making a wall of text. At most subtle weather/particles. No design annotations or large concept title.
Additional constraints: Do not add flavor-text slogans, quotes or decorative writing to scenery. Keep health bars proportionate to the displayed fractions, and keep required text clearly readable. No resemblance to an existing franchise. Make the theme visible in the interface materials as well as the world. All five card names and cost badges must be visible.
