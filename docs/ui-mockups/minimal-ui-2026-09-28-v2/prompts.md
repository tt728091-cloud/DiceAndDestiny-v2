# Minimal UI — second pass prompts

## Ground-level HUD

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE radically minimal Dice and Destiny combat screen, wide 16:9 landscape, high fidelity game screenshot. Input reference 1 is ONLY the game art/characters reference: rocky cave, mummy spellcaster with purple magic on left, floating skeletal Brine Mask on right. Do NOT copy reference 1's interface or spatial density. Input reference 2 is the PRIMARY COMPOSITION AND UI SCALE reference: Slay the Spire. Match its sparse battlefield, modest health bars directly beneath characters, tiny status badges, compact energy orb, and fanned hand tucked into bottom edge.
The previous attempt failed by creating huge HUD panels, sidebars, ability tiles, health bars, and rows of giant cards. This time the characters and cavern dominate. At least 75% of the frame is unobstructed environment/character art, with UI occupying isolated small patches near bottom and edges. No side panels, no full-width dock, no rectangular HUD backing, no big ability cards, no menu/dashboard appearance, no decorative borders, no long rule text in default view. Use the full width for a mostly EMPTY cavern with two distinct combatants left and right, facing each other. Large breathing room between them. Keep original realistic dark gothic character/cavern art, NOT Slay the Spire's cartoon characters.
Scale specifications relative to 1920x1080: characters about 390px tall, feet around y=730. EACH health bar is only 180–210px wide and 8–10px thick under feet, with small '24/24' and '16/16' legible text centered; small names 'Curse' and 'Brine Mask' directly above the bar, NOT large headers. Only a couple of tiny 18–22px status glyphs and stack counts immediately beneath health, no status panel, no 'Status 0' or 'No active statuses'. On enemy show tiny illustrative Curse skull '2' and Count hourglass '3'; player has no effects. These status values are mockup examples. Enemy energy is a tiny sparkle '1' next to its name. Player energy '3' is a small ~46px orb near hand/roll control.
Exactly FIVE player dice and exactly FIVE enemy dice remain on screen, ALWAYS visible but each only 32–38px square, the whole bank about 200px wide. Neutral unrolled faces use em dashes. Dice are tangible little ivory cubes with slight shadows, not huge empty dark slots. No D1/D2 labels or repeated 'clean' text; neutral styling denotes clean and die identity/state details available on focus.
Exactly FIVE cards persist at bottom center, gently fanned overlapping, combined hand about 600–720px wide, cards about 165px wide x 235px high, bottom tips cropped slightly by screen edge like Slay the Spire. Titles and cost gems remain visible; cards enlarge on focus for full rules, do not display a giant hovered card in this image. Card names 'Curse-Eater', 'Rotten Numeral', 'Black Tax', 'Hexward Retort', 'Grave Interest'; costs 0,2,1,0,0. Gothic mummy art on cards. Cards may have small rule text but do NOT scale up hand to show every paragraph simultaneously.
FOUR abilities are persistently accessible using four SMALL distinct glyphs (skull, shroud, bell, eclipse) with compact dice-pattern symbols / requirements and optional small names as specified in variant. Full names, requirements, complete rules appear on hover, click or controller focus, shown CLOSED here. No expanded tooltip. No text blocks.
Keep a modest 'Roll · 3/3' button and small 'Skip' text link. Round/phase is just 'Round 1 · Planning' in small quiet top-center text and five tiny progress dots (third gold); hovering exposes complete phase labels. Player deck/discard small pile glyphs with 19 and 0 at bottom corners of hand, removed 0 and hand 5 in tiny secondary glyph counts nearby. Enemy deck14 / hand2 / discard0 / removed0 a single very small glyph+count line near its health, no labels. Top-right ONLY four tiny utility glyphs for enemy abilities, log, inspect, settings with hover labels, no big labeled buttons. Retain information through icon counts and inspect interactions, not deleting mechanics.
Visual hierarchy: character silhouettes and cards first, dice second, health readable but restrained, everything else quiet. Ivory labels with subtle dark text shadow, tiny restrained gold accents, thin red HP bars. Never use a broad panel. Never enlarge UI to fill empty space. No desktop chrome, no title banner, no diagram annotations.

Variant 6: Closest to Slay the Spire. Characters at 25% and 75% of width. Their small health/status assemblies exactly below feet at 70% canvas height. Player five-dice bank at x=8–19%, y=84%, floating directly over cave floor, with small Roll button immediately below and energy orb beside it. Enemy five-dice bank directly below its tiny HP/status at x=70–81%, y=77%. FOUR small ability glyphs in a loose horizontal row above the player's dice near left-bottom, no tile borders, no full names. Five-card fan bottom center x=33–68%. Clear large empty middle. All HUD entirely borderless and localized.
