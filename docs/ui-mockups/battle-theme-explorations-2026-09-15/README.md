## Shared information

All explore the same pre-roll offensive planning state: Round 4; Venom 7/24 health, 1 energy, Bleed 2; Blade Warden 12/20 health, 5 energy, Poison 2. Four player abilities keep their visible hybrid number/symbol recipes. Five hand cards retain names and costs: Extract 0, Incubate 0, Coagulate 1, Shock Dose 2, Measured Dose 2. Roll, remaining rolls, Skip, phase, enemy-ability access, Log, Inspect and settings remain represented.

Needlefang has alternative 3/4/5-Fang tiers. Venom Gland requires two Gland and one Coil. Fever Spike has alternatives two Fang / one Gland / one Coil and two Fang / two Gland / one Coil. Terminal Bite requires three Coil. Information affordances indicate full effect detail on hover/focus/tap.

These concepts vary camera, materials, character interpretation, art style and UI treatment. Pile counters stay hidden. Dice appear during rolling rather than occupying permanent empty slots.

Generated graphics are visual concepts: deterministic health-bar proportions, final symbol designs, typography, responsive sizing and accessible interactions would be addressed in implementation. Decorative environment writing in some variants is concept flavor, not new rules.

## Suggested shortlist

- Living manuscript: strongest illustrated identity; the battle becomes a story unfolding on the page.
- Frozen runestone lake: high camera and large empty surface suited to animated physical dice.
- Celestial porcelain: calm, bright interface with minimal framing.
- Shadow-puppet theater: expressive character silhouettes and a coherent handmade style.
- Neon occult rooftop: a bolder departure that retains a tense duel atmosphere.
