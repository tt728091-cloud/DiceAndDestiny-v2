# Kingdom Death-inspired watercolor concept

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original characters and scene inspired by lantern-lit survival horror, stone-face terrain, bone, soot and sparse warm light. Static art-direction mockup; no game code changed. Generated UI repeats the energy values inside the icons and beside them; final UI should show each value once.

## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE polished widescreen 16:9 watercolor game screenshot concept for Dice and Destiny in a KINGDOM DEATH: MONSTER inspired dark fantasy theme. The user wants the game's eerie lantern-lit survival-horror art direction, grotesque organic forms, stark human vulnerability, bone and soot colors, and ominous sculptural environment, while keeping the watercolor medium established in prior concepts. An original scene and original character designs; do not reproduce a specific published illustration or logo.

ART DIRECTION AND COMPOSITION:
True richly textured watercolor and expressive fine ink, cold-press paper grain, granulating charcoal-black washes, chalk white, old ivory, tiny warm gold lantern flames, dark dried-crimson and venom-green accents. Painterly game artwork, not 3D miniatures, not a photograph. A wide elevated three-quarter view over a barren nightmarish plain made of gigantic smooth stone human faces and cracked black mineral ground. Faces form the terrain mostly near foreground corners and distant margins; the central dueling area is a broad quiet field of weathered pale stone, subtly contoured but unobstructed for future dice and effects. Background dissolves into an infinite ink-black void with a few distant tiny lantern lights. No cathedral, no castle, no forest, no standard courtyard. Unsettling sculptural forms, asymmetry, dream logic.
Venom at mid-left: small adult human survivor apothecary, short disheveled dark hair, exhausted expression, fully clothed in ragged cream wraps and a black hide shoulder cape, bone charms, green glass poison vial in one hand, warm lantern hanging at the belt; fragile silhouette, no conventional hooded mage.
Blade Warden at upper-right: small-on-screen yet imposing original humanoid nightmare swordsman, elongated ivory mask without eyes, antler-like branching calcified armor, black sinew between pale bone plates, tattered burgundy cloth, one massive irregular sword resting at an angle. Visibly monstrous, sculptural, not a standard armored knight. Exactly these two combatants. Atmospheric unease without explicit gore or nudity.
UI should feel like spare etched bone labels and ink-washed manuscript slips, high contrast, no decorative giant portraits or oversized frames. Small stacked abilities at lower-left below survivor, five illustrated compact cards along bottom center, roll controls lower-right. Top HUDs minimal. Most center is open. Polished, deliberate, beautiful visual hierarchy.
