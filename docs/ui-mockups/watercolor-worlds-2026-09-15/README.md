# Watercolor worlds

Ten watercolor battle concepts based on the selected Autumn Watercolor image. Created with the built-in image-generation tool using that image as a style and layout reference.

## Collection

1. [Moonlit winter](01-moonlit-winter.png) — A silent snow-covered monastery courtyard beside a frozen lake, distant pines and ruined bell tower, pale blue snow washes, deep indigo shadows and tiny warm amber lanterns along edges.
2. [Overgrown conservatory](02-overgrown-conservatory.png) — An abandoned cathedral-like botanical glasshouse, broken ironwork and tall translucent windows at the perimeter, enormous ferns and hanging vines framing the edges.
3. [Tidal cloister](03-tidal-cloister.png) — A ruined seaside cloister revealed at low tide, broad pale stone terrace with shallow still pools around its edges, distant turquoise sea and half-submerged arches.
4. [Poppy remembrance](04-poppy-remembrance.png) — A solemn ruined battlefield transformed into a red-poppy meadow, broad pale dirt clearing between two distant crumbling stone memorials, poppies framing the sides, misty hills.
5. [Desert mirage](05-desert-mirage.png) — A windswept oasis temple courtyard, distant rose-colored dunes and turquoise reflecting pool along far perimeter, elegant ruined sandstone arch at one side.
6. [Stormwatch lighthouse](06-stormwatch-lighthouse.png) — A broad pale headland courtyard beneath a distant old lighthouse, dark ocean and windswept cliffs beyond low boundary wall.
7. [Wisteria sanctuary](07-wisteria-sanctuary.png) — A forgotten garden sanctuary with violet wisteria cascading from ruined stone arches at outer edges, distant pale lake and mossy steps.
8. [Glowcap hollow](08-glowcap-hollow.png) — A mysterious subterranean mushroom grotto painted in true transparent watercolor, giant softly luminous mushrooms confined to sides and far background, broad open pale mineral shelf in center.
9. [Ash and embers](09-ash-and-embers.png) — A deserted volcanic monastery terrace above distant lava-lit mountains, broad pale ash-dusted flagstones center, broken black basalt pillars only at edges.
10. [Starfall orchard](10-starfall-orchard.png) — A mystical orchard on a high mountain terrace beneath a fading starry dawn, pale flowering pear trees only around edges, distant blue mountains and very faint gold constellation lines high in sky.

## Design continuity

Compact abilities remain stacked at lower-left, beside five cards. Symbol recipes, health, energy, statuses, phase, roll count and utility controls remain visible. The center stays open for future dice and effect animation. No card-pile counters.

Static visual concepts only; no game implementation was changed. Exact health-bar proportions and small recipe punctuation still need deterministic rendering during implementation. The Venom Gland recipe is two Gland symbols plus one Coil; small stray marks in the generated images are not subtraction operators. Full ability effects are intended to appear on hover.

Suggested shortlist: Wisteria Sanctuary for the closest continuation of Autumn, Overgrown Conservatory for its architecture, and Starfall Orchard for a magical setting.
