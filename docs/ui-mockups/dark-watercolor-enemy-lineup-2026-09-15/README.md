# Dark watercolor enemy lineup

Ten static encounter concepts using the approved face-free wasteland, Venom character and left-side dice/control layout. Created with built-in image generation, one reference-image edit per enemy. All opponent numbers are the same illustrative sample state, not proposed balance values. No game code changed.

## Encounters

1. [Bellback Pilgrim](01-bellback-pilgrim.png) — A hulking low quadrupedal creature whose back is a gigantic cracked iron pilgrim bell, supported by four long knuckled ivory limbs.
2. [Ashwing Matriarch](02-ashwing-matriarch.png) — An enormous grounded nocturnal moth-beast, two tall folded ivory wings rise like ragged burned vellum sails, patterns are abstract soot veins, NEVER eyes or faces.
3. [Reliquary Crab](03-reliquary-crab.png) — A massive asymmetrical six-legged crustacean with a weathered stone reliquary as its carapace, abstract cracked geometric carvings with no faces.
4. [Thorn Seamstress](04-thorn-seamstress.png) — A tall stooped four-armed nightmare seamstress in layers of torn grey linen, with a narrow smooth featureless ceramic head.
5. [Cinder Colossus](05-cinder-colossus.png) — A broad squat walking kiln golem assembled from cracked charcoal basalt blocks and fused old iron.
6. [Wax Revenant](06-wax-revenant.png) — A hovering slender apparition made of layered pale candle wax and hanging funeral cloth.
7. [Flint Tusk](07-flint-tusk.png) — A powerful low boar-like nightmare beast with a bristling ridge of black flint spines, heavy hide like wrinkled charcoal parchment, two immense uneven ivory tusks shaped like curved stone blades, and cracked bone armor grown over shoulders.
8. [Drowned Oracle](08-drowned-oracle.png) — A floating bell-shaped abyssal oracle creature with a translucent smoky-grey mantle, a crown of smooth broken ceramic plates, and long trailing black ribbon tendrils dangling above the dry ground.
9. [Carrion Harp](09-carrion-harp.png) — An original gaunt flightless avian creature with a tall arched back forming a natural harp-like bony frame strung with taut black fibrous strands.
10. [Glass Centipede](10-glass-centipede.png) — An enormous coiled centipede with overlapping smoky bottle-glass armor segments, tarnished bronze seams, dozens of fine ivory legs and a raised front section.

## Preserved design

Venom with lantern and vial, five unique dice above Roll/Skip, four compact abilities below, five cards along bottom, utilities lower-right, phase and status HUDs. Terrain has no carved human faces. Existing generated HUD conventions are retained from the approved reference.
