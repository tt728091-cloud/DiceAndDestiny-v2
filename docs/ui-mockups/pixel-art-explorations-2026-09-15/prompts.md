# Built-in image generation prompts

## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE complete widescreen 16:9 pixel-art game screenshot for Dice and Destiny. Entire image must be authentic consistent pixel art: environment, small character sprites, item illustrations, symbols, UI frames and readable pixel typography. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters at consistent scale, no blurry painted backgrounds, no photorealistic objects, no smooth 3D rendering, no mixed-resolution assets. Polished playable screenshot, not a mood board, collage, title screen or poster.
Same core offensive pre-roll planning information in every version:
Player "Venom", health "7/24", energy diamond "1", red blood-drop badge "2" labeled "Bleed".
Enemy "Blade Warden", health "12/20", energy diamond "5", green poison badge "2" labeled "Poison".
Two small full-body sprites facing across a generous open center for animated dice and effects. Venom is a poison alchemist/rogue; Blade Warden a sword-bearing guardian, themed below. Keep small HUDs adjacent to characters or in upper corners.
Compact phase track "Effects · Income · Offensive · Defensive · Damage", Offensive active, and "Round 4 · Planning".
Four SMALL readable abilities near the lower-left edge, with information affordance for hover details and these exact hybrid symbol recipes:
"Needlefang": 3 tooth icon / 4 tooth icon / 5 tooth icon.
"Venom Gland": two vial icons and one spiral icon.
"Fever Spike": two alternative rows. Row one: 2 tooth icon + 1 vial icon + 1 spiral icon. Row two: 2 tooth icon + 2 vial icon + 1 spiral icon.
"Terminal Bite": three spiral icons.
Use consistent distinct tooth/vial/spiral glyphs. Numbers and symbols only in recipe areas, no ingredient words. Ability names remain visible.
Five compact illustrated hand cards at bottom with exact names AND numeric cost badges: "Extract" 0, "Incubate" 0, "Coagulate" 1, "Shock Dose" 2, "Measured Dose" 2. These must all remain visible; card art should be pixelated too.
"Roll 5 dice", "3 rolls left", smaller "Skip". Small "Enemy abilities", "Log", "Inspect" and settings gear controls. No pile counters, no empty dice boxes or rolled dice yet, no extra combatants or invented stats. Half the screen or more should remain free of UI. Compact edge controls with high contrast, small attractive panels, no giant ability grid. Preserve a readable interface despite pixel styling. No decorative writing or slogans.
The following direction determines palette, camera, sprite design and UI skin.
